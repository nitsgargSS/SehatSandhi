English
-------
Namaste,

Sehatsandhi International brings patients from abroad (NRIs, Nepal, the Gulf, Africa, UK, USA and more) to checked hospitals across India. Patients compare hospitals by treatment, see your facilities, photos and package estimates, and choose. We look after their visa letter, airport pickup and stay.

Joining is free. Sehatsandhi earns a service fee only when a patient comes through us.

Who can join:
• Hospitals with in-patient beds (IPD) and an ICU, a doctor on duty 24x7
• NABH accreditation (full or entry-level), every licence valid
• An English-speaking coordinator on WhatsApp
• Registered on the Medical & Ayush Visa Portal (e-FRRO)
(Clinics and OPD-only practices cannot join.)

How to get listed:
1. Register your hospital: sehatsandhi.com/business/register (we switch on IPD for you)
2. Dashboard → International: fill your details, upload licences with expiry dates
3. Add at least 5 photos (building, room, ICU, OT) and your package prices
4. Accept the agreement and send for review
5. We verify every document and do a short video walkthrough — then you are listed

See the portal: sehatsandhi.com/international
Questions: WhatsApp 87087 18705
