Sehatsandhi International — Hospital agreement

Between NG Technologies (operating Sehatsandhi), 1743 Vishnu Garden, Jagadhri – 135003, Haryana, India (“Sehatsandhi”) and the hospital named below (“the hospital”), for patients from outside India whom Sehatsandhi brings to the hospital.

Terms (as accepted online)

  1. Joining is free. Sehatsandhi’s service fee is 10% on top of the hospital’s price for every international patient Sehatsandhi brings. It is a business-to-business fee, never paid to or by an individual doctor.
  2. Patients see the hospital’s submitted price plus Sehatsandhi’s fee, and are told the fee is included.
  3. Price promise: the price given to Sehatsandhi equals the hospital’s own international rate.
  4. Every quote and answer to a Sehatsandhi patient goes through Sehatsandhi. For 12 months the hospital does not deal with that patient around Sehatsandhi.
  5. The hospital tells the patient and Sehatsandhi in writing, with the reason, before any extra paid work (emergencies excepted). Without a written medical reason the final bill stays within 10% of the final quote.
  6. Service standards: reply to an enquiry within 24 hours, quote within 48 hours of reports, answer complaints within 48 hours.
  7. The hospital keeps every licence valid. An expired licence pauses new enquiries until it is renewed.
  8. NMC rules: no paid ranking, no testimonials, no before/after photos, no claims of guaranteed results.
  9. Medical care and its outcome are the hospital’s responsibility; Sehatsandhi arranges travel and coordination.
  10. Sehatsandhi may pause or remove a listing that breaks these terms.

Accepted on behalf of the hospital by the owner or an authorised signatory, who also confirms that the details and documents the hospital has submitted are true.

Annex 2 — What patients are told about prices (reference)

  1. The estimate is based on the reports you send. The final quote comes after the doctor examines you in person.
  2. The price can rise if the doctor finds something new: another condition, a complication, extra days, ICU, blood, or a change of implant or room.
  3. The hospital tells you and Sehatsandhi in writing, with the reason, before any extra paid work. Emergencies are the only exception, and the reason is written afterwards.
  4. Without a written medical reason, the final bill stays within 10% of the final quote.

Prices on the portal are shown as estimates only, including Sehatsandhi’s service fee; the final price is the hospital’s written quote.

Annex 3 — Listing requirements (reference)

  • A. Government licences — 15 items, each uploaded with its expiry date (hospital registration, fire NOC, building, bio-medical waste, pollution consents, AERB, drug licence, blood, oxygen, staff registrations, indemnity insurance, GST/PAN, and others where they apply)
  • B. NABH accreditation (full or entry-level, or full applied for) and government / insurer panels — these set the 4- or 5-star level
  • C. Clinical facilities — IPD beds, ICU with ventilators, 24x7 duty doctor, emergency, modular OT, CSSD, lab, radiology, pharmacy, blood, oxygen, power backup, infection control, records on Sehatsandhi
  • D. Comfort — private rooms for international patients, companion bed, Wi-Fi, food choices, accessibility, a checked hotel nearby
  • E. International desk — named coordinator, languages, registration on the Medical & Ayush Visa Portal (e-FRRO), foreigner reporting within 24 hours, written quotes, English consent forms, bills and discharge papers, follow-ups, complaints
  • F. Speciality extras — e.g. transplant centre registration (THOTA), ART registration for IVF, AERB for cath lab / radiotherapy
  • G. Photos — at least 5, including the building, a patient room, the ICU and the operation theatre

A listing opens only after every required document is uploaded, in date and verified by Sehatsandhi, the photos are approved, a video walkthrough is done and this agreement is accepted. An expired required licence pauses the listing automatically until the renewal is verified.

Annex 4 — Patient declaration for treatments with legal conditions (reference)

For organ transplant, bone marrow transplant and IVF, patients read the legal notes and accept:

  1. I have read the legal notes above and will follow them and all Indian laws that apply to my treatment.
  2. I will not take part in anything illegal — including paying for or selling an organ, giving false documents, or asking for a treatment the law does not allow.
  3. I understand that the hospital and the government committees decide whether the treatment can go ahead, and that Sehatsandhi only helps with information and travel.
  4. I understand that Sehatsandhi is not responsible for any wrongdoing, loss or legal consequence that comes from breaking Indian law, by me or anyone acting for me.
