==================================================================
RECORDING (phone)
==================================================================
- Vertical (9:16), 1080p or 4K, back camera, phone at eye level on a stand.
- Sit or stand in front of a plain wall; light from a window IN FRONT of you.
  I replace or blur the background, add titles, subtitles, music and the end card.
- Same shirt for a whole series, so the videos look like one set.
- Record each line as its own take. If you stumble, pause 2 seconds and say
  the whole line again — I keep the best take.
- Clip-on mic (₹500–1,000) makes the biggest difference. Otherwise stay within
  an arm's length of the phone, fans and AC off.
- 20–30 videos in one sitting is realistic: do one file at a time, a short break between files.
- Name the files after the video, e.g. "V2-1 take3.mov", or just say
  "V2.1" to the camera before each video.
